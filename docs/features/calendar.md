# Calendar

On a wide screen the whole month fits on one screen, its six weeks sharing the height below one header band, and each day shows the items that fit with "+N more" beside its number. Coming up stays for the phone agenda and the Week view, since the wide month already shows every day. A chosen day opens in a sheet: beside the grid on a wide screen, so every day stays clickable, and a bottom sheet on a phone. The open day is the only thing that opens the sheet. Today opens the Day view of today and puts focus on it without opening its sheet. Moving to a month or week that no longer shows the open day closes it, and switching between Month and Week keeps it when it is still shown. Escape closes only the innermost thing, so an open menu closes before the day.

The Day view is one day as an hour-by-hour timeline that fills the screen at every width and scrolls inside itself, never the page. It opens at 7 AM, or two hours before now when the now-line would otherwise be off screen. Timed meetings sit at their Central start with a height set by their length (30 minutes when Outlook gives no end), and overlapping meetings share the width side by side. Next steps, critical dates, tours and all-day meetings sit in a band above the timeline, since none of them has a time. A line marks the current time on today. Previous and Next move one day, the arrow keys move the focused day, and clicking an item opens its day sheet as in Month.

Dates whose day could not be read never take room under the grid: a small "N without a date" chip in the header opens their list in the sheet.

When a refresh fails, Calendar keeps the last dates, selected day, and Updated time and shows a retry notice; Retry refreshes the dates, while an expired session clears the screen.

Calendar also shows both partners' Outlook meetings, with Central start times, and planned tours on open deals, read with the dates for the shown month or week and again on every Refresh. A meeting both partners attend shows once with both names. Like Home, a meeting that runs past midnight or an all-day event that spans several days shows on its start day only. Meetings and tours on past days are settled, never overdue; only a next step past its due day shows as late. Like Home, a note appears only when a partner's calendar could not be read, last synced from Outlook more than 18 hours ago, or may be incomplete; a failed refresh keeps the meetings already shown with the same retry notice as the dates; the dates and next steps still show. The read sits behind the same Deals switch as the rest of Calendar; when the engine does not serve it, the page says nothing about meetings.
