# Calendar

When a refresh fails, Calendar keeps the last dates, selected day, and Updated time and shows a retry notice; Retry refreshes the dates, while an expired session clears the screen.

Calendar also shows both partners' Outlook meetings, with Central start times, and planned tours on open deals, read with the dates for the shown month or week and again on every Refresh. A meeting both partners attend shows once with both names. Like Home, a meeting that runs past midnight or an all-day event that spans several days shows on its start day only. Meetings and tours on past days are settled, never overdue; only a next step past its due day shows as late. Outlook only syncs about the next two weeks, so a quiet line says so, and a later day with no meetings does not mean the partner is free. Like Home, a note appears only when a partner's calendar could not be read, last synced from Outlook more than 18 hours ago, or may be incomplete; a failed refresh keeps the meetings already shown with the same retry notice as the dates; the dates and next steps still show. The read sits behind the same Deals switch as the rest of Calendar; when the engine does not serve it, the page says nothing about meetings.
